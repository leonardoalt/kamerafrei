      kamerafrei

      Routes through Berlin that stay out of the view of known surveillance cameras — computed on your device, shareable, offline-capable.

      How it works

      Camera positions and directions come from OpenStreetMap. Each camera gets a realistic visibility zone (view cones, blocked by buildings); the router finds paths that minimize the meters inside those zones. The avoidance level sets how much detour you accept.
